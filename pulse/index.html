Questions

Common questions.

Do I need to do anything each month?
No. Pulse works from the books your bookkeeper already closes. You just read the report and join the call.
Is my financial information kept confidential?
Yes. Your information is used only to prepare your reports and is never shared with anyone else. You'll sign a short authorization before anything is shared, so you're always in control.
Is this tax or accounting advice?
No. Pulse helps you understand and use your financial results. Tax and accounting questions stay with your tax preparer and bookkeeper.
What if I have a bigger question?
Bring it up on your call. Larger questions, like whether to hire, raise prices or expand, can be handled as a separate, fixed-price project.
