Questions we help answer

The answers are usually already in your records.

Your books, payroll and sales records hold far more than a profit and loss statement. A few of the questions owners ask most:

What does each employee really cost me?
Wages plus payroll taxes, benefits and overtime, compared with the work they support.
Which customers or jobs actually make money?
Profitability by customer, job, product or service line, not just in total.
Where has spending crept up?
Costs that have grown faster than revenue, and which ones are worth a closer look.
How does this year compare with last?
Month by month and season by season, so you know what's normal for your business.
Am I priced right?
Whether your pricing has kept pace with your costs and with the market.
Where is my cash going?
Timing of money in and out, past-due balances, and what to watch next.
